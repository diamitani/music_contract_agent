# Split Sheet — How to Use This Template & Questionnaire

Source template: `references/templates/split-sheet.md`

## When to use this document

Use immediately after a co-writing session, before memories fade or a track
starts circulating — this is the fastest, lowest-friction document in the
library. If the user asks for something quick "right after the session,"
this is almost always what they mean, not a full Songwriter Collaboration
Agreement.

## What this contract affects

- Composition/publishing ownership only (not the master recording)
- Registration with PROs (ASCAP/BMI/SESAC) and mechanical licensing

## Step 1 — Ask only what's missing

**Song & Session**
- Song title
- Date of the session
- Full legal names of everyone who contributed to the writing (melody, lyrics, topline, beat/instrumental if the beatmaker is being credited as a co-writer rather than a licensed beat purchase)

**Splits**
- Percentage for each writer — confirm the numbers sum to exactly 100%
- Each writer's role (helps resolve future disputes even though it's not required)
- Each writer's PRO affiliation (ASCAP/BMI/SESAC), if known — needed for royalty collection later, use `songwriter-split-sheet.md` (the detailed PRO-registration version) instead of this quick one if the user needs IPI/CAE numbers captured now

**Samples**
- Does the composition contain any sample, interpolation, or replay of another copyrighted work? If yes, this needs to be disclosed and cleared separately — the warranty in this template is not a substitute for actual clearance.

## Step 2 — Watch for these risk patterns

- **Yellow:** percentages don't add up to 100% — always catch this before finalizing.
- **Yellow:** a beatmaker who sold a beat under a separate license is listed as a co-writer without confirming the license actually grants a composition percentage.
- **Green:** simple, prompt splits documentation right after the session is the single best thing an independent artist can do to avoid future disputes — encourage it even for casual collaborations.

## Step 3 — Which version to use

- **Quick split sheet** (`split-sheet.md`): fast, informal, good for immediately after a session.
- **Detailed split sheet** (`songwriter-split-sheet.md`): includes PRO affiliation, publishing company info, CAE/IPI number, and lyrics-vs-music percentage breakdown — use this when the song is about to be registered with a PRO or released commercially.

## Delivery checklist

- [ ] Percentages sum to 100%
- [ ] Song title and date filled in
- [ ] All writer names spelled correctly and consistently
- [ ] Sample disclosure addressed (yes/no, and if yes, flagged for separate clearance)
- [ ] Signature line for every listed writer
