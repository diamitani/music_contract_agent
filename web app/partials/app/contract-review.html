Artist Management Agreement

Uploaded Aug 1, 2026 · 14 pages · 3 parties detected

In Review

1. Term and Territory

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for an initial period of three (3) years (the "Initial Term"), and shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

2. Manager's Commission

Manager shall receive a commission equal to twenty percent (20%) of all Gross Revenue derived from Artist's activities in the entertainment industry, including but not limited to recording, publishing, touring, merchandising, sponsorship, and endorsement income.

3. Expenses

Manager shall be entitled to reimbursement for reasonable out-of-pocket expenses incurred on Artist's behalf, subject to Artist's prior written approval for any single expense exceeding two hundred fifty dollars ($250.00).

4. Exclusivity

During the term of this Agreement, Artist shall engage Manager as Artist's exclusive personal manager worldwide. Artist shall not engage any other person or entity to perform management services without Manager's prior written consent.

5. Post-Term Commission

Following termination of this Agreement, Manager shall continue to receive commissions on all revenue derived from agreements entered into during the term, for a period of five (5) years post-termination, or for the life of such agreements, whichever is longer.

20% Commission Rate HIGH
Industry standard is 15–20% for a full-service manager. 20% is at the top of the range and should come with strong justification.
5-Year Post-Term Commission HIGH
Post-term commission tail is unusually long. Standard is 1–3 years post-term or tied to agreements still in effect. This could mean paying commissions on deals you negotiate yourself years later.
Auto-Renewal Clause MEDIUM
Auto-renewal without a performance benchmark means you could be locked in even if the manager underperforms.
Expense Approval Threshold LOW
$250 approval threshold is reasonable. Protects you from surprise expenses.
