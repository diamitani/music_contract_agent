Producer / Composer Agreement

Draft v2 · 8 pages

1. Parties

This Producer / Composer Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of [Date] by and between:

Marcus Avery, professionally known as "MAV" ("Artist"), and

Dominic Reyes ("Producer").

2. Services

Producer agrees to provide production, composition, arrangement, and recording services for the musical work titled "Midnight Drive" (the "Master Recording").

3. Compensation

Flat Fee: Artist shall pay Producer a one-time fee of $2,500.00 upon execution of this Agreement.

Royalty: Producer shall receive 18% of net royalties received by Artist from exploitation of the Master Recording across all formats.

4. Ownership

Artist shall retain one hundred percent (100%) ownership of the Master Recording and all copyrights therein. Producer's contribution is a work made for hire to the extent permitted by law.

5. Credit

Producer shall receive credit as "Produced by Dominic Reyes" on all releases of the Master Recording.
