# Producer Royalties Agreement — How to Use This Template & Questionnaire

Source template: `references/templates/producer-royalties-agreement.md`

## When to use this document

Use this agreement when the deal involves: Producer and Artist/Label.

## What this contract type affects

- Royalty point structure
- Accounting and audit rights
- Credit

## Step 1 — Find out who's asking

Confirm which side of the deal the user represents (Producer and Artist/Label). Artist-protective
defaults in this skill are written from the perspective of the independent artist/
creator side of the deal. If the user is the other party (the hiring/paying side),
say so plainly and adjust: still be fair and accurate, but don't silently apply
artist-favoring defaults to someone who isn't the artist without telling them.

## Step 2 — Ask only what's missing

Use these grouped questions. Skip anything already answered in the conversation,
uploaded files, or pasted messages.

**Parties**
- Full legal names of all parties (and stage/business names)
- Notice addresses/emails
- Entity/signatory authority, if a company or LLC is involved

**Project & Money**
- Royalty rate (points), typically 2-5% of net receipts for a non-featured producer
- Whether points are 'all-in' (paid from the artist's royalty) or paid on top by the label
- Recoupment: are producer points subject to recoupment of the artist's advance, or paid from first dollar?

**Rights**
- Confirms this is a royalty arrangement, not ownership of the master

**Control & Risk**
- Accounting frequency (quarterly/semi-annual) and statement/audit rights
- Credit requirements
- What happens to points if the track is remixed, re-released, or used in a compilation

## Step 3 — Watch for these risk patterns

**Yellow:** no defined accounting schedule or audit right — producer has no way to verify they're being paid correctly.
**Yellow:** points defined as 'all-in' without the producer understanding that means splitting the artist's own royalty, not extra money from the label.

## Contract-specific questions to ask

- What point rate did the producer and artist/label agree to?
- Are the points 'all-in' (from the artist's share) or paid separately by the label?
- How often will royalty statements be sent, and does the producer want audit rights?

## Delivery checklist

- [ ] All placeholders replaced (no blank underscores or `[BRACKETS]` left in the final draft)
- [ ] Money terms consistent everywhere they appear in the document
- [ ] Rights grant does not contradict the ownership section
- [ ] Term/termination/renewal dates don't conflict
- [ ] Signature blocks match the named parties exactly
- [ ] Attorney-review notice included per `references/05-safety-and-legal-boundaries.md`
- [ ] If any risk flag above is Red, the attorney-review recommendation is called out explicitly in the delivery, not buried in the fine print
