# Booking / Talent Agent Agreement — How to Use This Template & Questionnaire

Source template: `references/templates/booking-agent-agreement.md`

## When to use this document

Use this agreement when the deal involves: Artist and Agent/Agency.

## What this contract type affects

- Commission on live performance income
- Territory and exclusivity of the booking right
- Term

## Step 1 — Find out who's asking

Confirm which side of the deal the user represents (Artist and Agent/Agency). Artist-protective
defaults in this skill are written from the perspective of the independent artist/
creator side of the deal. If the user is the other party (the hiring/paying side),
say so plainly and adjust: still be fair and accurate, but don't silently apply
artist-favoring defaults to someone who isn't the artist without telling them.

## Step 2 — Ask only what's missing

Use these grouped questions. Skip anything already answered in the conversation,
uploaded files, or pasted messages.

**Parties**
- Full legal names of all parties (and stage/business names)
- Notice addresses/emails
- Entity/signatory authority, if a company or LLC is involved

**Project & Money**
- Commission rate on gross performance fees (industry norm ~10-20%)
- Who pays deposit/travel/rider costs and whether they're deducted before or after commission
- Minimum guarantee or fee floor the agent must secure, if any

**Rights**
- Agent is granted the right to solicit and negotiate performance offers on artist's behalf — not ownership of anything

**Control & Risk**
- Exclusive vs. non-exclusive, and for which territory (city/region/country/worldwide)
- Whether the artist must run other offers through the agent or can self-book certain shows
- Term and notice period to terminate
- Whether the agent needs artist sign-off before accepting an offer, or has standing authority up to a certain fee

## Step 3 — Watch for these risk patterns

**Yellow:** worldwide exclusivity when the agent only actually has relationships in one region.
**Yellow:** no minimum activity requirement — agent can sit on exclusive rights without booking anything.
**Red flag:** agent given authority to accept offers and bind the artist without prior approval.

## Contract-specific questions to ask

- What territory does this agent actually work — local, regional, national, international?
- Should the artist be able to book shows themselves without commission, or is everything exclusive?
- What's the agreed commission percentage, and is it before or after travel/production costs?
- Is there a minimum number of shows/income the agent must deliver to keep the exclusive?

## Delivery checklist

- [ ] All placeholders replaced (no blank underscores or `[BRACKETS]` left in the final draft)
- [ ] Money terms consistent everywhere they appear in the document
- [ ] Rights grant does not contradict the ownership section
- [ ] Term/termination/renewal dates don't conflict
- [ ] Signature blocks match the named parties exactly
- [ ] Attorney-review notice included per `references/05-safety-and-legal-boundaries.md`
- [ ] If any risk flag above is Red, the attorney-review recommendation is called out explicitly in the delivery, not buried in the fine print
