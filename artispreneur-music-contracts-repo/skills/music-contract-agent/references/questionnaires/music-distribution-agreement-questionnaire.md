# Music Distribution Agreement — How to Use This Template & Questionnaire

Source template: `references/templates/music-distribution-agreement.md`

## When to use this document

Use this agreement when the deal involves: Artist/Label and Distributor.

## What this contract type affects

- Distribution rights scope (which platforms, territory)
- Revenue share
- Ownership (artist retains masters)

## Step 1 — Find out who's asking

Confirm which side of the deal the user represents (Artist/Label and Distributor). Artist-protective
defaults in this skill are written from the perspective of the independent artist/
creator side of the deal. If the user is the other party (the hiring/paying side),
say so plainly and adjust: still be fair and accurate, but don't silently apply
artist-favoring defaults to someone who isn't the artist without telling them.

## Step 2 — Ask only what's missing

Use these grouped questions. Skip anything already answered in the conversation,
uploaded files, or pasted messages.

**Parties**
- Full legal names of all parties (and stage/business names)
- Notice addresses/emails
- Entity/signatory authority, if a company or LLC is involved

**Project & Money**
- Distributor's fee/revenue share percentage
- Accounting frequency

**Rights**
- Confirms the artist keeps ownership of masters and compositions — distribution is a service, not a rights transfer, unless the user is dealing with a distributor unusually asking for more

**Control & Risk**
- Exclusive vs. non-exclusive distribution rights
- Takedown rights and how fast the artist can pull a release
- Whether this covers one release or an ongoing catalog relationship

## Step 3 — Watch for these risk patterns

**Red flag:** a 'distributor' asking for ownership of masters or an equity stake in the recordings — that's no longer a distribution deal, it's a label deal, and should be evaluated as one (see Artist/Label Recording Agreement).
**Yellow:** no defined accounting schedule — distributors should report and pay on a predictable cycle.

## Contract-specific questions to ask

- Is this for one release or an ongoing relationship covering future releases too?
- What's the distributor's fee/revenue share, and how often will they pay out?
- Does the artist want the ability to pull the release down quickly if needed?

## Delivery checklist

- [ ] All placeholders replaced (no blank underscores or `[BRACKETS]` left in the final draft)
- [ ] Money terms consistent everywhere they appear in the document
- [ ] Rights grant does not contradict the ownership section
- [ ] Term/termination/renewal dates don't conflict
- [ ] Signature blocks match the named parties exactly
- [ ] Attorney-review notice included per `references/05-safety-and-legal-boundaries.md`
- [ ] If any risk flag above is Red, the attorney-review recommendation is called out explicitly in the delivery, not buried in the fine print
