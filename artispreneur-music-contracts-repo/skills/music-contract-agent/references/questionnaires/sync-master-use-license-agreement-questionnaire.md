# Sync / Master Use License Agreement — How to Use This Template & Questionnaire

Source template: `references/templates/sync-master-use-license-agreement.md`

## When to use this document

Use this agreement when the deal involves: Licensor (Artist/Label/Publisher) and Licensee (Production Company/Brand/Platform).

## What this contract type affects

- Master use license scope
- Sync (composition) license scope
- Territory, term, and media

## Step 1 — Find out who's asking

Confirm which side of the deal the user represents (Licensor (Artist/Label/Publisher) and Licensee (Production Company/Brand/Platform)). Artist-protective
defaults in this skill are written from the perspective of the independent artist/
creator side of the deal. If the user is the other party (the hiring/paying side),
say so plainly and adjust: still be fair and accurate, but don't silently apply
artist-favoring defaults to someone who isn't the artist without telling them.

## Step 2 — Ask only what's missing

Use these grouped questions. Skip anything already answered in the conversation,
uploaded files, or pasted messages.

**Parties**
- Full legal names of all parties (and stage/business names)
- Notice addresses/emails
- Entity/signatory authority, if a company or LLC is involved

**Project & Money**
- Sync fee (flat, one-time is typical) and whether it covers both master and publishing or just one
- Back-end royalties (if the project generates ongoing revenue, e.g., a TV show in syndication)

**Rights**
- Exact scope: which project, which cut/edit, what media (TV, streaming, social, ads), what territory, and for how long (perpetuity vs. defined term)
- Most-favored-nations clause if multiple rights holders are involved (all get equal terms)

**Control & Risk**
- Approval rights over how the music is used (contextual approval — e.g., not paired with content the artist finds objectionable)
- Credit requirements

## Step 3 — Watch for these risk patterns

**Red flag:** 'in perpetuity, throughout the universe, all media' grant for a flat one-time fee with no cap on how the music can be reused later (sequels, spin-offs, ads).
**Yellow:** no most-favored-nations clause when both the master and publishing sides need separate approval — one side could undercut the other's rate.

## Contract-specific questions to ask

- What specific project, episode/scene, or campaign is this for?
- What media and territory does the licensee need (broadcast, streaming, social, paid ads, worldwide vs. one country)?
- Is this a flat fee, or does the artist want a share of backend revenue (e.g., ad revenue on a viral campaign)?
- Does the artist have any restrictions on context (e.g., not political ads, not competitor brands)?

## Delivery checklist

- [ ] All placeholders replaced (no blank underscores or `[BRACKETS]` left in the final draft)
- [ ] Money terms consistent everywhere they appear in the document
- [ ] Rights grant does not contradict the ownership section
- [ ] Term/termination/renewal dates don't conflict
- [ ] Signature blocks match the named parties exactly
- [ ] Attorney-review notice included per `references/05-safety-and-legal-boundaries.md`
