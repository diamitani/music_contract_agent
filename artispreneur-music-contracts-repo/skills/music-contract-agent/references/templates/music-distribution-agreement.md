MUSIC DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Music Distribution Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of [DATE],
by and between [ARTIST/LABEL NAME] ("Artist") and [DISTRIBUTOR NAME]
("Distributor").

1. Grant of Rights. Artist grants Distributor a non-exclusive [or:
exclusive, if negotiated] right to reproduce, distribute, market, and
make available the recording(s) titled [RELEASE TITLE(S)] (the
"Recordings") through digital streaming and download platforms
(including but not limited to Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music,
YouTube Music, and TikTok) within the Territory of [worldwide /
TERRITORY], for the Term of this Agreement.

2. Ownership. Artist retains full ownership of the copyright in the
Recordings and the underlying compositions. Distributor obtains no
ownership interest — only the limited rights granted in Section 1, solely
for the purpose of distribution and associated promotion.

3. Term. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for
[1 year / until terminated], automatically renewing for successive
[1-year] terms unless either party gives written notice of
non-renewal at least [30/60] days before the end of the then-current
term.

4. Revenue Share. Distributor will collect royalties generated by the
Recordings from platforms and pay Artist [___]% of Net Receipts (as
defined below), retaining [___]% as Distributor's fee. "Net Receipts"
means all amounts actually received by Distributor from platforms
attributable to the Recordings, after deduction only of documented,
platform-charged fees (not general Distributor overhead).

5. Accounting. Distributor will provide Artist with a royalty statement
and payment at least [quarterly], showing streams/downloads by platform
and territory where reasonably available, and gross and net amounts.
Artist may request supporting documentation for any statement, and
Distributor will provide it within [30] days.

6. Delivery and Metadata. Artist is responsible for delivering final,
release-ready audio files, artwork, and metadata (title, artist name,
songwriter/publisher splits, ISRC/UPC if available) to Distributor at
least [X] days before the intended release date. Distributor is not
responsible for release delays caused by late or incomplete delivery.

7. Takedown Rights. Artist may request that Distributor remove the
Recordings from any or all platforms at any time upon written notice.
Distributor will process such requests within [10] business days, subject
to each platform's own takedown timelines, which are outside Distributor's
control.

8. Term for New Releases. This Agreement covers only the Recordings
listed above unless the parties agree in writing to add additional
releases under the same terms.

9. Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement for the other
party's uncured material breach upon [15/30] days' written notice and
opportunity to cure. Upon termination, Distributor will take down the
Recordings from all platforms within [30] days and pay any Net Receipts
already collected but not yet paid.

10. No Exclusivity to Other Releases. Unless Section 1 grants an
exclusive right, this Agreement does not restrict Artist from
distributing other, different releases through any other distributor.

11. Warranties. Artist warrants ownership of, or authority to distribute,
the Recordings, and that they do not infringe any third party's rights.

12. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of
[STATE].
