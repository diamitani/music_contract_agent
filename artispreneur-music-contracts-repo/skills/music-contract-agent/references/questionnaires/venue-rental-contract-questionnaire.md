# Venue Rental Contract — How to Use This Template & Questionnaire

Source template: `references/templates/venue-rental-contract.md`

## When to use this document

Use this agreement when the deal involves: Renter (Artist/Producer/Event Organizer) and Venue Owner.

## What this contract type affects

- Rental fee and dates
- Permitted use and capacity
- Damage deposit and liability

## Step 1 — Find out who's asking

Confirm which side of the deal the user represents (Renter (Artist/Producer/Event Organizer) and Venue Owner). Artist-protective
defaults in this skill are written from the perspective of the independent artist/
creator side of the deal. If the user is the other party (the hiring/paying side),
say so plainly and adjust: still be fair and accurate, but don't silently apply
artist-favoring defaults to someone who isn't the artist without telling them.

## Step 2 — Ask only what's missing

Use these grouped questions. Skip anything already answered in the conversation,
uploaded files, or pasted messages.

**Parties**
- Full legal names of all parties (and stage/business names)
- Notice addresses/emails
- Entity/signatory authority, if a company or LLC is involved

**Project & Money**
- Rental fee, deposit, and payment schedule
- Additional costs (security, cleaning, overtime hours)

**Rights**
- Confirms a space-rental relationship — not a co-promotion or revenue-share deal unless explicitly added

**Control & Risk**
- Set-up/breakdown windows, capacity limits, and permitted activities (alcohol, filming, ticketing)
- Insurance requirements (event insurance, certificate of insurance naming the venue)
- Damage deposit refund conditions

## Step 3 — Watch for these risk patterns

**Yellow:** no cap on additional/overtime fees — can balloon the cost after the event.
**Yellow:** insurance requirement not specified — confirm what coverage and limits the venue requires before signing.

## Contract-specific questions to ask

- What are the exact dates/times needed, including setup and breakdown?
- What's the rental fee, deposit, and what does the deposit cover/how is it refunded?
- Does the renter need to provide a certificate of insurance?
- What activities need to be explicitly permitted (alcohol service, filming, ticket sales)?

## Delivery checklist

- [ ] All placeholders replaced (no blank underscores or `[BRACKETS]` left in the final draft)
- [ ] Money terms consistent everywhere they appear in the document
- [ ] Rights grant does not contradict the ownership section
- [ ] Term/termination/renewal dates don't conflict
- [ ] Signature blocks match the named parties exactly
- [ ] Attorney-review notice included per `references/05-safety-and-legal-boundaries.md`
- [ ] If any risk flag above is Red, the attorney-review recommendation is called out explicitly in the delivery, not buried in the fine print
