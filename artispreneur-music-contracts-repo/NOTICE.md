## Important — read before making this repository public

This library contains two categories of contract template, and they have
**different provenance**:

### 1. Original templates (safe to open-source under this repo's license)

Six templates were drafted from scratch for this project, synthesized from
publicly available descriptions of standard industry clause structures —
not copied from any specific third-party document:

- Exclusive Beat License Agreement
- Non-Exclusive Beat License Agreement
- NDA / Non-Circumvention Agreement
- Music Distribution Agreement
- Merchandise License Agreement
- Independent Contractor Agreement (general)

These are marked `status: original` in their YAML records
(`skills/music-contract-agent/references/contracts/*.yaml`) and are covered
by this repository's license without qualification.

### 2. Sourced templates (provenance unconfirmed — verify before redistributing)

Twenty-four templates were extracted from `.doc`/`.docx` files supplied
during this project's development. **The original source, authorship, and
license of those uploaded files is not known to this project.** They may
have come from a purchased template pack, a free download, a course
resource, or elsewhere — that history wasn't preserved in the files
themselves. These are marked `status: sourced` in their YAML records.

**Before you publish this repository publicly, or represent these 24
templates as freely reusable by others, confirm you actually hold — or
have permission to grant — redistribution rights to them.** If you can't
confirm that, you have a few reasonable options:

- Keep the repository **private** (internal use only, or your team/site),
  which sidesteps the redistribution question entirely.
- **Remove or replace** the sourced templates before making the repo
  public, keeping only the six original ones plus the skill
  infrastructure (schema, scripts, workflow instructions) — all of which
  are safe to open-source.
- **Rewrite** the sourced templates from scratch the way the six original
  ones were built (synthesized from generally known clause structures,
  not copied from a specific document), and re-mark them `status:
  original` once that's done.
- If you know the actual origin and have the right to redistribute them
  (e.g. you personally authored them, or purchased a license that
  explicitly permits redistribution), document that here and proceed.

This isn't a hypothetical concern — it's the difference between "I built a
useful open tool" and "I redistributed someone else's paid template pack
without permission." Take five minutes to trace where these files
originally came from before flipping the repo to public.
