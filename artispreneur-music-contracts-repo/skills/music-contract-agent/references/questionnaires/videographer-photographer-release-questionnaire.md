# Videographer / Photographer Release — How to Use This Template & Questionnaire

Source template: `references/templates/videographer-photographer-release.md`

## When to use this document

Use this agreement when the deal involves: Artist/Subject and Videographer/Photographer.

## What this contract type affects

- Ownership of resulting photos/video
- License back to the subject for their own use
- Usage rights for the creator (portfolio, licensing to others)

## Step 1 — Find out who's asking

Confirm which side of the deal the user represents (Artist/Subject and Videographer/Photographer). Artist-protective
defaults in this skill are written from the perspective of the independent artist/
creator side of the deal. If the user is the other party (the hiring/paying side),
say so plainly and adjust: still be fair and accurate, but don't silently apply
artist-favoring defaults to someone who isn't the artist without telling them.

## Step 2 — Ask only what's missing

Use these grouped questions. Skip anything already answered in the conversation,
uploaded files, or pasted messages.

**Parties**
- Full legal names of all parties (and stage/business names)
- Notice addresses/emails
- Entity/signatory authority, if a company or LLC is involved

**Project & Money**
- Flat fee, if any, for the shoot

**Rights**
- Who owns the resulting images/footage — typically the photographer/videographer owns the copyright while granting the subject a license to use it personally/promotionally
- Whether the creator can license the images to third parties (stock, press) or only use for portfolio

**Control & Risk**
- Can the subject request approval before public use of specific images?
- Credit requirements for the photographer/videographer

## Step 3 — Watch for these risk patterns

**Yellow:** no license-back to the subject — artist should always retain the right to use their own photos/video for promo.
**Yellow:** creator granted rights to sell/license images to third parties without artist approval, when that wasn't the intent.

## Contract-specific questions to ask

- What's the shoot for (music video, promo photos, behind-the-scenes)?
- Does the artist need approval rights before images/footage are used publicly?
- Can the photographer/videographer license these to others, or is use limited to this project and the artist's own promo?

## Delivery checklist

- [ ] All placeholders replaced (no blank underscores or `[BRACKETS]` left in the final draft)
- [ ] Money terms consistent everywhere they appear in the document
- [ ] Rights grant does not contradict the ownership section
- [ ] Term/termination/renewal dates don't conflict
- [ ] Signature blocks match the named parties exactly
- [ ] Attorney-review notice included per `references/05-safety-and-legal-boundaries.md`
- [ ] If any risk flag above is Red, the attorney-review recommendation is called out explicitly in the delivery, not buried in the fine print
