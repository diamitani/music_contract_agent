# Brand Sponsorship Agreement — How to Use This Template & Questionnaire

Source template: `references/templates/brand-sponsorship-agreement.md`

## When to use this document

Use this agreement when the deal involves: Artist and Brand/Sponsor.

## What this contract type affects

- Endorsement scope and exclusivity
- Use of name/image/music
- Term and territory

## Step 1 — Find out who's asking

Confirm which side of the deal the user represents (Artist and Brand/Sponsor). Artist-protective
defaults in this skill are written from the perspective of the independent artist/
creator side of the deal. If the user is the other party (the hiring/paying side),
say so plainly and adjust: still be fair and accurate, but don't silently apply
artist-favoring defaults to someone who isn't the artist without telling them.

## Step 2 — Ask only what's missing

Use these grouped questions. Skip anything already answered in the conversation,
uploaded files, or pasted messages.

**Parties**
- Full legal names of all parties (and stage/business names)
- Notice addresses/emails
- Entity/signatory authority, if a company or LLC is involved

**Project & Money**
- Sponsorship fee structure and payment schedule
- Performance bonuses/milestones, if any (streams, event attendance, social metrics)

**Rights**
- What exactly is licensed — name, likeness, music, social content — and for what use (events, advertising, product packaging)
- Term length and renewal terms

**Control & Risk**
- Category exclusivity (artist can't endorse a competing brand)
- Creative approval over how the brand uses the artist's name/image
- Morals/reputation clause — mutual, not one-sided

## Step 3 — Watch for these risk patterns

**Red flag:** one-sided morals clause that lets the brand terminate for any reputational issue, with no equivalent protection for the artist if the brand does something damaging.
**Yellow:** broad category exclusivity with no carve-out for the artist's existing deals.

## Contract-specific questions to ask

- What's the campaign scope — single activation, ongoing partnership, tour sponsorship?
- What's the fee and payment schedule, and are there performance bonuses?
- What exclusivity is the brand asking for, and does it conflict with any existing deals?
- Does the artist want a morals clause to be mutual (either side can exit for cause)?

## Delivery checklist

- [ ] All placeholders replaced (no blank underscores or `[BRACKETS]` left in the final draft)
- [ ] Money terms consistent everywhere they appear in the document
- [ ] Rights grant does not contradict the ownership section
- [ ] Term/termination/renewal dates don't conflict
- [ ] Signature blocks match the named parties exactly
- [ ] Attorney-review notice included per `references/05-safety-and-legal-boundaries.md`
- [ ] If any risk flag above is Red, the attorney-review recommendation is called out explicitly in the delivery, not buried in the fine print
