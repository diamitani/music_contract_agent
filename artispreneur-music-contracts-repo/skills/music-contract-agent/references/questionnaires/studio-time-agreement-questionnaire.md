# Studio Time Agreement — How to Use This Template & Questionnaire

Source template: `references/templates/studio-time-agreement.md`

## When to use this document

Use this agreement when the deal involves: Artist/Client and Studio/Engineer.

## What this contract type affects

- Booked hours/sessions
- Ownership of resulting recordings
- Cancellation/no-show policy

## Step 1 — Find out who's asking

Confirm which side of the deal the user represents (Artist/Client and Studio/Engineer). Artist-protective
defaults in this skill are written from the perspective of the independent artist/
creator side of the deal. If the user is the other party (the hiring/paying side),
say so plainly and adjust: still be fair and accurate, but don't silently apply
artist-favoring defaults to someone who isn't the artist without telling them.

## Step 2 — Ask only what's missing

Use these grouped questions. Skip anything already answered in the conversation,
uploaded files, or pasted messages.

**Parties**
- Full legal names of all parties (and stage/business names)
- Notice addresses/emails
- Entity/signatory authority, if a company or LLC is involved

**Project & Money**
- Hourly or block rate, deposit, and payment due timing
- Overtime rate if sessions run long

**Rights**
- Confirms the client (artist) owns the resulting masters unless the studio explicitly retains any interest (e.g., in-house engineer/producer taking points)

**Control & Risk**
- Cancellation/no-show/late policy and any fees
- File delivery format and timeline after the session
- Studio's right to use session as demo reel/portfolio (should require artist consent)

## Step 3 — Watch for these risk patterns

**Yellow:** studio claims any ownership interest in the recordings by default — should be an explicit, separate negotiation (see Producer Royalties Agreement), not baked into a simple rental.
**Green:** clear cancellation policy protects both sides.

## Contract-specific questions to ask

- How many hours/sessions are booked, and at what rate?
- Is an engineer/producer included, and do they expect any royalty interest beyond the hourly rate?
- What's the cancellation/no-show policy?
- How and when will final files be delivered?

## Delivery checklist

- [ ] All placeholders replaced (no blank underscores or `[BRACKETS]` left in the final draft)
- [ ] Money terms consistent everywhere they appear in the document
- [ ] Rights grant does not contradict the ownership section
- [ ] Term/termination/renewal dates don't conflict
- [ ] Signature blocks match the named parties exactly
- [ ] Attorney-review notice included per `references/05-safety-and-legal-boundaries.md`
- [ ] If any risk flag above is Red, the attorney-review recommendation is called out explicitly in the delivery, not buried in the fine print
