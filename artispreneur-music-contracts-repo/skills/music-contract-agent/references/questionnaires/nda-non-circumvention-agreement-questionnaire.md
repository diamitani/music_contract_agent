# NDA / Non-Circumvention Agreement — How to Use This Template & Questionnaire

Source template: `references/templates/nda-non-circumvention-agreement.md`

## When to use this document

Use this agreement when the deal involves: Party A and Party B (mutual — both sides may disclose confidential info).

## What this contract type affects

- Confidentiality of unreleased material or deal info
- Non-circumvention of introduced contacts
- No IP license granted by this document itself

## Step 1 — Find out who's asking

Confirm which side of the deal the user represents (Party A and Party B (mutual — both sides may disclose confidential info)). Artist-protective
defaults in this skill are written from the perspective of the independent artist/
creator side of the deal. If the user is the other party (the hiring/paying side),
say so plainly and adjust: still be fair and accurate, but don't silently apply
artist-favoring defaults to someone who isn't the artist without telling them.

## Step 2 — Ask only what's missing

Use these grouped questions. Skip anything already answered in the conversation,
uploaded files, or pasted messages.

**Parties**
- Full legal names of all parties (and stage/business names)
- Notice addresses/emails
- Entity/signatory authority, if a company or LLC is involved

**Project & Money**
- Typically no payment terms — this is a protective document, not a deal itself

**Rights**
- Confirms this grants NO license or ownership of anything — it only protects information and relationships while a deal is being discussed

**Control & Risk**
- Duration of confidentiality (2-3 years is typical) and non-circumvention (1-2 years)
- Whether this is mutual (both sides protected) or should be one-directional

## Step 3 — Watch for these risk patterns

**Green:** signing this before sharing an unreleased track, deal terms, or a business introduction is a low-cost, high-value habit — encourage it even for casual-seeming conversations.
**Yellow:** if only one party is actually disclosing sensitive information, consider whether a one-way (unilateral) version is more appropriate than mutual — ask which direction the sensitive info is flowing.

## Contract-specific questions to ask

- What's actually being protected — an unreleased song, a business plan, deal terms, a contact introduction?
- Is information flowing both ways (mutual) or mostly one direction (should be unilateral instead)?
- Is there a specific introduction (a contact, a potential deal) that needs non-circumvention protection, and for how long?

## Delivery checklist

- [ ] All placeholders replaced (no blank underscores or `[BRACKETS]` left in the final draft)
- [ ] Money terms consistent everywhere they appear in the document
- [ ] Rights grant does not contradict the ownership section
- [ ] Term/termination/renewal dates don't conflict
- [ ] Signature blocks match the named parties exactly
- [ ] Attorney-review notice included per `references/05-safety-and-legal-boundaries.md`
- [ ] If any risk flag above is Red, the attorney-review recommendation is called out explicitly in the delivery, not buried in the fine print
