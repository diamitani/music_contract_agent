# Artist Booking Agreement (Show/Event) — How to Use This Template & Questionnaire

Source template: `references/templates/artist-booking-agreement.md`

## When to use this document

Use this agreement when the deal involves: Artist and Promoter/Buyer.

## What this contract type affects

- Performance fee
- Rider (technical/hospitality)
- Cancellation and force majeure

## Step 1 — Find out who's asking

Confirm which side of the deal the user represents (Artist and Promoter/Buyer). Artist-protective
defaults in this skill are written from the perspective of the independent artist/
creator side of the deal. If the user is the other party (the hiring/paying side),
say so plainly and adjust: still be fair and accurate, but don't silently apply
artist-favoring defaults to someone who isn't the artist without telling them.

## Step 2 — Ask only what's missing

Use these grouped questions. Skip anything already answered in the conversation,
uploaded files, or pasted messages.

**Parties**
- Full legal names of all parties (and stage/business names)
- Notice addresses/emails
- Entity/signatory authority, if a company or LLC is involved

**Project & Money**
- Guaranteed fee, deposit, and balance due date
- Percentage of door/ticket sales, if applicable, and how it's verified
- Who covers travel/lodging

**Rights**
- Confirms this is a one-off (or short run) live performance service, not an ongoing exclusive booking relationship

**Control & Risk**
- Set time and length
- Merch selling rights at the venue and any commission the venue takes
- Recording/broadcast rights for the performance — does the artist retain rights to any recording of the show?

## Step 3 — Watch for these risk patterns

**Yellow:** no deposit required — increases risk of a no-pay cancellation.
**Yellow:** broad recording/broadcast rights grant to the venue/promoter with no artist approval or compensation for later use.

## Contract-specific questions to ask

- What's the date, venue, fee, and payment structure (flat guarantee vs. door split)?
- What deposit is required and when is the balance due?
- Does the promoter want to record or livestream the show? If so, on what terms?
- What are the merch terms at this show?

## Delivery checklist

- [ ] All placeholders replaced (no blank underscores or `[BRACKETS]` left in the final draft)
- [ ] Money terms consistent everywhere they appear in the document
- [ ] Rights grant does not contradict the ownership section
- [ ] Term/termination/renewal dates don't conflict
- [ ] Signature blocks match the named parties exactly
- [ ] Attorney-review notice included per `references/05-safety-and-legal-boundaries.md`
- [ ] If any risk flag above is Red, the attorney-review recommendation is called out explicitly in the delivery, not buried in the fine print
