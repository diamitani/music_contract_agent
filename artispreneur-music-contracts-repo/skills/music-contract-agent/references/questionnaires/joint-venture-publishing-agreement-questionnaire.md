# Joint Venture Publishing Agreement — How to Use This Template & Questionnaire

Source template: `references/templates/joint-venture-publishing-agreement.md`

## When to use this document

Use this agreement when the deal involves: Publisher and Record Company / Distributor / Other JV partners.

## What this contract type affects

- Joint venture name and purpose
- Profit/loss sharing percentages
- Capital contributions

## Step 1 — Find out who's asking

Confirm which side of the deal the user represents (Publisher and Record Company / Distributor / Other JV partners). Artist-protective
defaults in this skill are written from the perspective of the independent artist/
creator side of the deal. If the user is the other party (the hiring/paying side),
say so plainly and adjust: still be fair and accurate, but don't silently apply
artist-favoring defaults to someone who isn't the artist without telling them.

## Step 2 — Ask only what's missing

Use these grouped questions. Skip anything already answered in the conversation,
uploaded files, or pasted messages.

**Parties**
- Full legal names of all parties (and stage/business names)
- Notice addresses/emails
- Entity/signatory authority, if a company or LLC is involved

**Project & Money**
- Cash and property contributed by each partner
- Percentage of net profit/loss per partner

**Rights**
- This creates a business entity/relationship, not a simple license — treat as high-stakes and recommend attorney/accountant review by default given ongoing shared liability

**Control & Risk**
- How new partners can be admitted (should require unanimous consent)
- What happens on a partner's death, bankruptcy, or exit — does the JV continue?
- Decision-making process for the JV's business

## Step 3 — Watch for these risk patterns

**Always recommend attorney review.** Joint ventures create ongoing shared liability and profit obligations — this is not a one-off contract, it's a business relationship.
**Red flag:** no clear exit/buyout mechanism if one partner wants out or the relationship breaks down.

## Contract-specific questions to ask

- Who are all the partners, and what is each contributing (cash, catalog, distribution, services)?
- What percentage of profit/loss does each partner receive?
- Has the user considered forming an actual LLC for liability protection instead of an informal joint venture? (often the better artist-protective default — see the LLC Operating Agreement template)

## Delivery checklist

- [ ] All placeholders replaced (no blank underscores or `[BRACKETS]` left in the final draft)
- [ ] Money terms consistent everywhere they appear in the document
- [ ] Rights grant does not contradict the ownership section
- [ ] Term/termination/renewal dates don't conflict
- [ ] Signature blocks match the named parties exactly
- [ ] Attorney-review notice included per `references/05-safety-and-legal-boundaries.md`
- [ ] If any risk flag above is Red, the attorney-review recommendation is called out explicitly in the delivery, not buried in the fine print
