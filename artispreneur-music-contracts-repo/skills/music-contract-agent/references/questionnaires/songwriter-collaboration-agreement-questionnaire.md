# Songwriter Collaboration Agreement — How to Use This Template & Questionnaire

Source template: `references/templates/songwriter-collaboration-agreement.md`

## When to use this document

Use this agreement when the deal involves: Co-writers.

## What this contract type affects

- Publishing/composition ownership split
- Administration and collection
- Credit

## Step 1 — Find out who's asking

Confirm which side of the deal the user represents (Co-writers). Artist-protective
defaults in this skill are written from the perspective of the independent artist/
creator side of the deal. If the user is the other party (the hiring/paying side),
say so plainly and adjust: still be fair and accurate, but don't silently apply
artist-favoring defaults to someone who isn't the artist without telling them.

## Step 2 — Ask only what's missing

Use these grouped questions. Skip anything already answered in the conversation,
uploaded files, or pasted messages.

**Parties**
- Full legal names of all parties (and stage/business names)
- Notice addresses/emails
- Entity/signatory authority, if a company or LLC is involved

**Project & Money**
- Ownership percentage per writer
- Who administers/collects (self, publisher, PRO) and how collected money is distributed

**Rights**
- Confirms this only covers the composition/publishing, not any master recording made of it

**Control & Risk**
- Approval rights for licensing the song (samples, sync, cover versions) — unanimous or majority?
- What happens if one writer wants to license the song and another objects

## Step 3 — Watch for these risk patterns

**Yellow:** no default rule for what happens if writers can't agree on a license request — can freeze the song indefinitely.
**Green:** simple percentage splits with each writer independently able to license their own share (standard and artist-friendly) if that's the intent — confirm it's actually wanted.

## Contract-specific questions to ask

- What are the agreed percentage splits between writers?
- Should any single writer be able to approve licenses on their own for their share, or does everyone need to agree?
- Is a formal split sheet also needed for PRO registration? (recommend yes)

## Delivery checklist

- [ ] All placeholders replaced (no blank underscores or `[BRACKETS]` left in the final draft)
- [ ] Money terms consistent everywhere they appear in the document
- [ ] Rights grant does not contradict the ownership section
- [ ] Term/termination/renewal dates don't conflict
- [ ] Signature blocks match the named parties exactly
- [ ] Attorney-review notice included per `references/05-safety-and-legal-boundaries.md`
- [ ] If any risk flag above is Red, the attorney-review recommendation is called out explicitly in the delivery, not buried in the fine print
