# Production Agreement (Producer / Production Company) — How to Use This Template & Questionnaire

Source template: `references/templates/production-agreement.md`

## When to use this document

Use this agreement when the deal involves: Artist and Production Company/Producer.

## What this contract type affects

- Master ownership
- Development/production term
- Shopping/label-deal rights

## Step 1 — Find out who's asking

Confirm which side of the deal the user represents (Artist and Production Company/Producer). Artist-protective
defaults in this skill are written from the perspective of the independent artist/
creator side of the deal. If the user is the other party (the hiring/paying side),
say so plainly and adjust: still be fair and accurate, but don't silently apply
artist-favoring defaults to someone who isn't the artist without telling them.

## Step 2 — Ask only what's missing

Use these grouped questions. Skip anything already answered in the conversation,
uploaded files, or pasted messages.

**Parties**
- Full legal names of all parties (and stage/business names)
- Notice addresses/emails
- Entity/signatory authority, if a company or LLC is involved

**Project & Money**
- Recording budget and who fronts it
- Recoupment of production costs from future deals
- Producer's percentage of any deal secured (development/shopping fee)

**Rights**
- Does the production company own the masters during development, with a plan to shop to labels, or is this artist-owned with a paid producer?
- What happens to ownership if the production company secures a label deal — do they keep a piece as a production/JV deal?

**Control & Risk**
- Exclusivity during the development term
- Term length and what happens if no deal is secured
- Approval rights over which labels/deals the artist can accept

## Step 3 — Watch for these risk patterns

**Red flag:** production company takes perpetual master ownership plus a large cut of any future label deal, with no time limit on the exclusivity.
**Yellow:** vague 'reasonable efforts' shopping obligation with no deadline — artist can be locked up indefinitely with no deal materializing.

## Contract-specific questions to ask

- Is the goal to get shopped to a label, self-release, or both?
- Who is funding the recording — the production company, the artist, or split?
- If a label deal is secured, what percentage or role does the production company keep?
- What's the outside date — if no deal happens by [date], do all rights revert to the artist?

## Delivery checklist

- [ ] All placeholders replaced (no blank underscores or `[BRACKETS]` left in the final draft)
- [ ] Money terms consistent everywhere they appear in the document
- [ ] Rights grant does not contradict the ownership section
- [ ] Term/termination/renewal dates don't conflict
- [ ] Signature blocks match the named parties exactly
- [ ] Attorney-review notice included per `references/05-safety-and-legal-boundaries.md`
- [ ] If any risk flag above is Red, the attorney-review recommendation is called out explicitly in the delivery, not buried in the fine print
