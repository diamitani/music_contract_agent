MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AND NON-CIRCUMVENTION AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made as of [DATE], by and between [PARTY A NAME]
("Party A") and [PARTY B NAME] ("Party B"), collectively the "Parties,"
in connection with a possible business relationship involving [BRIEF
DESCRIPTION — e.g., "an unreleased song, a management arrangement, a
potential label deal"] (the "Purpose").

1. Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" means any
non-public information disclosed by either Party in connection with the
Purpose, including but not limited to: unreleased music, lyrics,
recordings, artwork, marketing plans, deal terms, contact lists,
financial information, and business strategies. Confidential Information
does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available
through no fault of the receiving Party; (b) was already known to the
receiving Party before disclosure; (c) is independently developed without
use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information; or (d) is
required to be disclosed by law or court order (with prompt notice to the
disclosing Party where legally permitted).

2. Non-Disclosure Obligations. Each Party agrees to: (a) keep the other
Party's Confidential Information confidential and use it only for the
Purpose; (b) not disclose it to any third party without prior written
consent, except to employees, contractors, or advisors who need to know
it for the Purpose and are bound by similar confidentiality obligations;
and (c) protect it with at least the same degree of care used to protect
its own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care.

3. Non-Circumvention. Each Party agrees not to bypass the other Party in
order to directly contact, negotiate with, or enter into an agreement
with any artist, label, business contact, investor, or other party
introduced by the other Party in connection with the Purpose, for a
period of [1-2] years from the date of introduction, without the
introducing Party's written consent or agreed compensation for the
introduction.

4. No License or Ownership Transfer. Nothing in this Agreement grants
either Party any license, ownership interest, or right to use the other
Party's music, name, likeness, trademarks, or other intellectual property,
beyond what is necessary to evaluate the Purpose.

5. Term. The confidentiality obligations in Section 2 continue for
[2-3] years from the date of this Agreement, or until the Confidential
Information ceases to qualify as confidential under Section 1, whichever
is earlier. The non-circumvention obligation in Section 3 continues for
the period stated there.

6. Return or Destruction. Upon written request, each Party will return or
destroy all materials containing the other Party's Confidential
Information, except for one archival copy retained solely for legal
compliance purposes.

7. No Obligation to Proceed. Nothing in this Agreement obligates either
Party to enter into any further business relationship, and either Party
may end discussions regarding the Purpose at any time.

8. Remedies. Each Party acknowledges that a breach of Sections 2 or 3 may
cause irreparable harm not fully compensable by monetary damages, and
that the non-breaching Party may seek injunctive relief in addition to
any other available remedies.

9. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of
[STATE].

10. Entire Agreement. This Agreement is the entire understanding between
the Parties regarding confidentiality and non-circumvention for the
Purpose, and supersedes any prior discussions on that subject.
